About

Erin Corbett

she/her

Erin is a musician, sound designer, game developer and 3D animator based in Toronto, Canada. For over a decade, she has worked across disciplines such as games, music, animation, audiobooks, and more.

After graduating from Metalworks Institute's Audio Production & Engineering program in 2012, she relocated to Toronto to begin her career as a freelance musician, audio engineer, sound designer, and visual artist. Since then, she has taken on creative roles in stage plays, indie video games, studio and live recordings, and audiobooks.

In 2016 Erin was the lead sound designer on the 2016 Toronto Best-of-Fringe show Like A Generation, and has also worked as a songwriter and producer with indie artists Leucrocuta, Ensh, R Flex, Brooklyn Doran, and Bears and Children.

In addition to her audio career, Erin has developed a unique approach to interactive visual art. Through the use of 3D graphics software Blender, Unreal, and Unity, she creates abstract digital landscapes and interactive experiences to accompany her musical output. Erin's visual portfolio includes album art for record label Glue Gun Records, Biblioteka, visuals for Wolfgang Gray (featured at A/V showcase Ponyhaus), and music videos for artists Martian Crisis Unit, Pink Snail, Faiyaz And The Wasted Chances, and many others.

Erin has also released a number of independent video games, including Terrorbone (2017) and Simpborne (2020), and is currently working on the survival horror game Helpless, Helpless, Helpless which has received grants from the Ontario Arts Council and Canada Council for the Arts.
